# Desktop homepage / Concept C

Open `index.html` through a static server. Local review: http://127.0.0.1:8766/

Original `c.html`, `shared.css`, and `shared.js` are preserved unchanged. The homepage uses separate `homepage.css` and `homepage.js`.

## Design decisions

- Retained the shutter split, salon reveal, editorial title and native reversible scroll. Extended the journey to 330vh, with the stone mask complete at 96% so it settles before leaving the sticky stage.
- Carried the same stone colour into the brand statement, replacing the end-of-study footer with the homepage.
- Sticky Nails typography beside two staggered photo stories; services use one changing image with scroll, hover and keyboard focus support.
- Staggered location portraits, direct booking/explore/directions links, one client quotation, mixed-proportion lookbook, dark booking close. No autoplay carousel.
- Restrained one-time reveals after the opening. Content is accessible HTML, visible without JavaScript. Reduced motion shows the opening title and ritual statement in normal flow and removes reveals/crossfades/sticky storytelling.
- Verified services: manicure, pedicure, Shellac polish, Bio Gel, acrylic, eyelash extensions, facial/body hair removal, facials. BIAB/Builder and an unverified dedicated brow category were omitted. This is a curated homepage, not the whole service catalogue.
