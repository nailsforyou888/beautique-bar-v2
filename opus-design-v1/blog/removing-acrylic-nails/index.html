How to remove acrylic nails at home

Without wrecking your natural nails.

Quick answer

Quick answer

File off the shiny top coat, soak your nails in 100% acetone for 20–30 minutes, then gently push the softened acrylic off with a cuticle pusher. Moisturise after. Expect it to take 45–60 minutes.

Time at home
45–60 minutes, both hands tied up
You’ll need
100% acetone, cotton pads, foil, a file, a cuticle pusher, petroleum jelly, cuticle oil

Step by step ↓

Step by step

Five steps, about an hour

  1. 1. Clip and file

    Clip your nails short, then file the shiny top layer until it looks dull. This lets the acetone soak in.

  2. 2. Protect your skin

    Put petroleum jelly on the skin around each nail. Acetone is very drying.

  3. 3. Soak and wrap

    Soak cotton pads in 100% acetone, press one on each nail, and wrap the fingertip in a square of foil.

  4. 4. Wait, then push gently

    Wait 20–30 minutes. When the acrylic has gone soft, push it off gently with a cuticle pusher. If it resists, wrap it again for another 5–10 minutes.

  5. 5. Buff and moisturise

    Buff away any small bits left over, then use cuticle oil and hand cream.

Where it goes wrong

Three mistakes to avoid

Forcing it off

Peeling or prying the acrylic before it has softened can tear off layers of your natural nail. If it doesn’t lift easily, soak longer.

Soaking too long

Acetone dries out your skin and cuticles. Protect the skin first and moisturise as soon as you’re done.

Rushing it

Most people underestimate the time. It takes close to an hour, and both hands are busy the whole time.

Questions

Acrylic removal FAQ

How do I remove acrylic nails at home?

File off the shiny top coat, soak your nails in 100% acetone for 20–30 minutes, then gently push the softened acrylic off with a cuticle pusher. Moisturise after. Expect it to take 45–60 minutes.

Does removal damage my nails?

Done properly, no. Damage usually comes from peeling or picking acrylic off at home before it has softened.

How long does acrylic removal take at the salon?

About 15 minutes, or about 30 minutes for the gentle soak-off removal.

How much is acrylic removal at Beautique Bar?

Acrylic removal is $20 at Yonge & York Mills and from $15 at Bridlewood Mall.

Can I remove Bio Gel the same way?

Yes. Bio Gel softens faster than acrylic, so soak for 10–15 minutes instead.
