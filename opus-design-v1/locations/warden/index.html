Questions

Good to know

Do you take walk-ins?

Yes. Walk-ins are welcome. Booking ahead secures your preferred time and technician.

What are your hours?

Mon–Fri 10 am – 8 pm. Sat 9 am – 7 pm. Sun 11 am – 6 pm.

How do I book?

Book online with Fresha, call 647-770-5232, or message us on WhatsApp.

Where are you?

2900 Warden Avenue, Scarborough, ON. Unit 244, on the 2nd floor of Bridlewood Mall, by the library.

Do you do manicures and pedicures for men?

Yes. A Gentleman's Pedicure is $33.

Do you do refills?

Yes. Crystal Gel / Acrylic Refill $50+, Bio Gel Refill $50+, Gel-X Refill $55+.

Why do some prices say “+”?

Those are starting prices. The total depends on length, design and add-ons, and your technician will confirm the price before your service.

How do I cancel or reschedule?

Please let us know as early as possible so we can offer the time to another guest: call 647-770-5232.

What if I’m not happy with my service?

Tell us before you leave so we can make it right.
