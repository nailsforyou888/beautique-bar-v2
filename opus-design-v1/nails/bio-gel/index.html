Bio Gel

A gel that looks and feels close to your natural nail — as an overlay on your own nails or as a full set with tips.

Options

How to book it

Overlay or full set

An overlay strengthens your natural nails; a full set with tips adds length. Both are finished in the colour or design you choose.

Refills

Refills fill in the growth so the set keeps its shape. Book them on the same menu.

Gel-X

Both salons also offer Gel-X full sets and refills.

Removal

Artificial nail removal is available at both salons.
