Gel-X

Soft-gel tips that cover the whole nail, shaped to the length you want and finished in any colour or design. At both salons.

At a glance

What to know before you book

What it is

Each Gel-X tip covers the whole nail. It is bonded with gel, cured under a lamp, then shaped and finished, so you get length without a sculpted acrylic set.

Full set

Bridlewood Mall: from $65. Yonge & York Mills: from $70. Length and design change the final price.

Refills

A refill fills in the growth so the set stays even. Most clients book one every three to four weeks.

Finishes

French, chrome, ombré and custom designs can be added. See nail art.

Questions

Good to know

How much is a Gel-X full set?

A Gel-X full set is from $65 at Bridlewood Mall and from $70 at Yonge & York Mills. The final price depends on length and design, and your technician confirms it before starting.

How much is a Gel-X refill?

A Gel-X refill is from $55 at Bridlewood Mall and from $55 at Yonge & York Mills.

How often do I need a refill?

Most clients come back every three to four weeks, as the natural nail grows out under the set. Your technician can suggest timing for your nails.

Gel-X or Bio Gel: which should I choose?

Gel-X uses full-cover tips, so it is the way to add length. Bio Gel is usually applied over your own nails for strength, or with tips if you want length too. If you are unsure, ask your technician at the start of your appointment.

Can I add nail art to Gel-X?

Yes. French, chrome and designs can be added to any set. See the nail art page for how design pricing works.

Do you remove Gel-X?

Yes. Artificial nail removal is from $15 at Bridlewood Mall and $20 at Yonge & York Mills.
