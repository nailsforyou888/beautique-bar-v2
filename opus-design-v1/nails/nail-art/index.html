Questions

Good to know

How much does nail art cost?

Designs are priced by detail and added to your manicure or set. At Bridlewood Mall, French or simple designs start from $5; at Yonge & York Mills, chrome, ombré or unicorn finishes are $15. Your technician confirms the price before starting.

Can I bring a picture of the design I want?

Yes. Send it to us on WhatsApp before your visit so we can tell you the price and how much time to book.

Can I get nail art on my natural nails?

Yes. Designs can be added to a Shellac manicure, or to a set such as Gel-X, Bio Gel or acrylic if you want length.

Do both salons do nail art?

Yes. French and chrome finishes are on the menu at both salons, and design add-ons are on the Bridlewood Mall menu. For a hand-painted or detailed design, send us a photo first so we can confirm the price and the salon.
