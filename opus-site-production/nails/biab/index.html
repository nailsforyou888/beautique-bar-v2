Who it suits

Questions

Good to know

What is BIAB?

BIAB is a builder gel: a thicker layer of gel that strengthens your natural nails without adding length. Our Bridlewood Mall menu lists it as Strong Shellac.

How much is BIAB?

BIAB is $40 on its own, or $50 with a spa manicure, at Bridlewood Mall.

Is BIAB the same as Shellac?

No. Shellac is a thin gel polish for colour. BIAB is a thicker builder gel that adds strength, which helps if your nails bend or break easily.

Can I get BIAB at Yonge & York Mills?

BIAB is on the Bridlewood Mall menu only. At Yonge & York Mills, a Bio Gel overlay is the closest option.

How long does BIAB last?

Most clients rebook every three to four weeks as the nail grows out. Your technician can suggest timing for your nails.
