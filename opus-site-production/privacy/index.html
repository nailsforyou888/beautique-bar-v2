Privacy Policy.

Legal

Last updated: 28 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Beautique Bar (“we”, “us”), operating salons at 3430 Yonge Street, Toronto and 2900 Warden Avenue (Bridlewood Mall), Scarborough, collects, uses and safeguards personal information when you visit beautiquebar.com or contact us to book an appointment.

Information we collect

Information you give us. If you call, text, email or message us on social media, we receive what you choose to share, such as your name, phone number, email address and appointment details.

Information collected automatically. When you use this website, our analytics and advertising tools may collect your IP address, device and browser type, approximate location, the pages you view, how you arrived on the site, and which buttons or links you select (for example Book, Call, Text, Directions or Email). This information is collected using cookies and similar technologies.

We do not collect payment information through this website.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiries and manage your appointments.
  • To understand how the website is used and to improve it.
  • To measure and improve our advertising, including showing our ads to people who have visited our site.
  • To keep the website secure and working properly.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

This website uses the following Google services, loaded through Google Tag Manager:

  • Google Analytics 4 to measure visits and interactions, such as page views and clicks on our booking, call, text, directions and email links.
  • Google Ads conversion tracking and remarketing to measure which of our ads lead to bookings and enquiries, and to show our ads to people who previously visited the site.

These services set cookies or use identifiers on your device and send information to Google, which may process it outside Canada, including in the United States. Google’s use of this information is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

You can control this in several ways: change your browser settings to block or delete cookies; use Google’s Ads Settings to opt out of personalized advertising; or install the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. Blocking cookies will not stop you from using the site.

Booking with Fresha

Online bookings are made through Fresha, a third-party booking platform. When you select Book, you leave our website and go to Fresha, where the information you enter is collected and handled under Fresha’s own privacy policy and terms. We receive the details of your booking so we can serve you.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with the service providers described above (Google and Fresha), with providers who host and secure this website, and where required by law.

How we protect it

This website is served securely over HTTPS. We keep enquiry and booking information only as long as needed to assist you or as required by law.

Your choices and rights

You may ask to see, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or withdraw your consent to how we use it, by contacting us at beautiquebar.yonge@gmail.com (Yonge & York Mills) or beautiquebar88@gmail.com (Bridlewood Mall). If you would rather not receive promotional messages from us, tell us and we will stop.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email beautiquebar.yonge@gmail.com (Yonge & York Mills) or beautiquebar88@gmail.com (Bridlewood Mall), or reach out via Instagram.

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version, with its date, always appears on this page.
