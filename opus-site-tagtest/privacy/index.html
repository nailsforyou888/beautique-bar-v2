Privacy Policy.

Legal

This Privacy Policy explains how Beautique Bar (“we”, “us”) collects, uses and safeguards the personal information you provide when you visit our website or book an appointment.

Information we collect

We do not collect payment information through this website.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiries and booking requests.
  • To provide and improve our services.
  • To contact you about your appointments when you ask us to.

How we protect it

This website is served securely over HTTPS. We retain enquiry information only as long as needed to assist you.

Third-party services

We may use privacy-respecting analytics and spam protection (such as Cloudflare Turnstile) to keep the site secure and understand how it is used. We do not sell your personal information.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of the personal information you have shared with us by contacting us at info@beautiquebar.com.

Contact us

Questions about this policy? Email info@beautiquebar.com or reach out via Instagram.

This policy may be updated from time to time; the latest version always appears on this page.
